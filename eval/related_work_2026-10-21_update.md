# Newer literature-grounded research agents, benchmarks and citation checkers (July 2025 to October 2026)

Update to `related_work_2026-10-20.md`, compiled 2026-10-21 by a web-research subagent. Dates and URLs were checked on a primary page unless listed under "Could not verify".

## Systems and benchmarks

1. **Kosmos** (Edison Scientific, FutureHouse spinout), Nov 2025. AI scientist that runs up to 12 h per objective, reads about 1,500 papers and executes about 42k lines of code per run; 79.4% of report statements judged accurate by independent scientists. New: a structured world model shared between a literature agent and a data agent across 200+ rollouts; every report statement is cited to code or primary literature. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824
2. **FutureHouse platform** (Crow, Falcon, Owl, Phoenix; Robin hypothesis loops), May 2025; **paper-qa** moved to calendar versioning Dec 2025 with hybrid sparse plus dense retrieval and a contradiction-detection setting. https://www.futurehouse.org/research-announcements/launching-futurehouse-platform-ai-agents , https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13400 , https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa
3. **Ai2 Asta, AstaBench, Asta Resources**, Aug to Oct 2025 (AstaBench at ICLR 2026). 2,400+ problems, 11 benchmarks, 57 agents, cost reported alongside accuracy; Asta v0 tops it at 53%. Asta Resources exposes a 225M-paper literature graph and a 12M full-text snippet index over MCP, with a date cutoff for benchmark runs. Ai2 publishes weekly citation telemetry for Asta answers (0.11% of citations to retracted papers). https://allenai.org/blog/astabench , https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21652 , https://allenai.org/asta/resources , https://allenai.org/blog/asta-citations
4. **SciArena** (Yale, NYU, Ai2), July 2025, NeurIPS 2025. Pairwise human voting on literature-grounded long-form answers: 47 models, 20k+ votes; LLM judges agree with humans about 65% of the time. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01001
5. **ToolUniverse** (Zitnik lab, Harvard), Sept 2025, v3 Aug 2026. 2,700+ scientific tools and 130 skills behind one interface; generates and optimizes tool specifications from natural language. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23426
6. **Biomni** (Stanford), preprint May 2025, Science July 2026 (DOI seen only in secondary coverage); Biomni-R0 model Oct 2025. General biomedical agent with tools, databases and protocols mined from the literature; 400+ tasks. https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.05.30.656746v1
7. **Deep-research benchmarks with citation metrics.** DeepResearch Bench (June 2025, ICLR 2026): FACT metric extracts statement-URL pairs and judges support, reporting citation accuracy and effective citations, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763 . BrowseComp-Plus (Aug 2025): a fixed curated corpus with human-verified evidence documents and hard negatives, so retriever and reasoner can be evaluated separately, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06600 . ResearchRubrics (Scale AI, Nov 2025, ICLR 2026): 2,593 expert-written rubric criteria; best agents under 68% compliance, https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07685
8. **Google Co-Scientist**, Nature, 19 May 2026 (preprint Feb 2025). Generate, Reflect, Rank (Elo tournament), Evolve, Proximity and Meta-review agents under a Supervisor; wet-lab validations in AML, liver fibrosis and phage biology; the Reflection agent searches for prior work before claiming novelty. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y , https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18864
9. **Medical agents and benchmarks.** MedProbeBench (Apr 2026): guideline-generation tasks scored with 1,200+ rubrics plus 5,130 atomic claims verified against cited evidence, 17 agents tested, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18418 . DeepMed (Jan 2026, ACL Findings 2026): a medical deep-research agent trained on multi-hop PubMed search, https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18496 . otto-SR (systematic-review agent, medRxiv June 2025) is not publicly released, https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.13.25329541
10. **Causal inference tooling.** CauSciBench (ICML 2026): 300+ queries over the full pipeline; the bottleneck is causal method selection and it worsens on real data, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/acharya26a.html . Causal AI Scientist (ICML 2026): decision-tree method selection with post-selection diagnostics, https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/74805 . THESEUS (Yonsei, medRxiv Mar 2026): LLMs turn methods text into JSON target-trial specifications and Strategus R code; 0.91 to 0.98 study-level accuracy on OHDSI studies, far lower on non-OHDSI full methods, https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.13.26348306
11. **2026 citation-fabrication audits.** GhostCite (Feb 2026): 13 LLMs fabricate 14 to 95% of citations; 2.2M citations in 56k papers audited, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06718 . Naser (Feb 2026): 69,557 citations from 10 LLMs, 11 to 57% hallucinated; citations agreed by three or more models are 95.6% real, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03299 . Zhao, Ginsparg, Yin et al. (May 2026): 146,932 hallucinated citations in 2025 papers across arXiv, bioRxiv, SSRN and PMC, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07723 . Topaz et al., Lancet (May 2026): 1 in 277 PubMed-indexed papers in early 2026 carries a fabricated reference (primary page returned 403; figures via https://retractionwatch.com/2026/05/07/one-in-277-pubmed-indexed-papers-in-2026-shows-fabricated-references-says-analysis/ ). Topaz et al. (Sept 2026): 26 LLMs, 55% of biomedical references fabricated; refusal is the best-performing behavior, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14988
12. **Citation checkers.** CiteAudit (Feb 2026): benchmark plus a four-stage verifier (metadata extraction, memory lookup, web retrieval, judgment), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23452 . Rao, Wong, Callison-Burch (Apr 2026): 3 to 13% of deep-research citation URLs are hallucinated; open tool `urlhealth`; agentic self-correction drops non-resolving URLs under 1%, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03173 . Badalova and Mayr (Jul 2026): five public checkers, none reliable unattended, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22693
13. **Zotero and Claude Code tooling.** zotero-mcp: 41 tools, full text, annotations, optional semantic search, Zotero 7 local API, https://github.com/54yyyu/zotero-mcp . K-Dense scientific-agent-skills: 177 Claude Code skills including paper lookup over 10 databases and pyzotero, no citation-verification skill, https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills . paper-search-mcp (PubMed, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex), https://github.com/openags/paper-search-mcp . Scite MCP connectors for Claude and ChatGPT (Aug 2026, paid), https://scite.ai/blog/august-2026-release-notes . Claude for Life Sciences (Oct 2025) ships PubMed and bioRxiv connectors, https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-life-sciences
14. **Commercial search.** Consensus Pro Search and Scholar Agent (Oct 2025) and Deep Search over saved libraries (2026), vendor claims unverified. Elicit Systematic Review is PRISMA 2020 compliant, https://support.elicit.com/en/articles/15018527-elicit-systematic-review-now-supports-prisma-2020-guidelines . PaSa (Jan 2025) predates the window, https://github.com/bytedance/pasa

## What Miguel should adopt from the 2025 to 2026 work

1. **Atomic-claim support check** after drafting: split the answer into claims, pair each with its quote, label supported / partial / unsupported (DeepResearch Bench FACT; MedProbeBench). Cheap.
2. **Existence check for any reference outside the Zotero corpus** via Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar, with Naser's multi-sample consensus rule; refuse rather than guess, since Topaz 2026 shows refusal beats recall. Cheap.
3. **Frozen-corpus probe with a date cutoff and a recorded retriever** so closed-book vs open-book deltas are attributable (BrowseComp-Plus; AstaBench Standard Tools). Cheap.
4. **Expert rubrics plus cost per answer** for 20 to 30 canonical causal questions (ResearchRubrics; AstaBench). Cheap to write, moderate to maintain.
5. **Cited method-selection decision tree with assumption diagnostics** (Causal AI Scientist; CauSciBench), each branch tagged with a corpus source. Cheap.
6. **Structured claims ledger across sessions** (Kosmos world model) wrapping the verified-quote memory. Costly.
7. **Hybrid dense plus BM25 retrieval and a contradiction mode** (paper-qa). Costly.
8. **Reflection pass before asserting novelty or absence of evidence** (Co-Scientist), routed to Asta or Scite with an explicit outside-corpus tag. Cheap.
