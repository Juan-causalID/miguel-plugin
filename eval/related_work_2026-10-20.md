# Comparable projects to Miguel and what they justify (survey, 2026-10-20)

Compiled by a web-research subagent; every entry was checked against a primary page (arXiv, publisher, GitHub, vendor docs) unless listed under "Could not verify". No causal-inference-specific literature agent was found; the closest neighbours are single-field RAG agents in biology and medicine plus citation-reliability studies.

## Projects

1. **PaperQA2 / LitQA2 / LAB-Bench** (FutureHouse, 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13740 , https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10362 , https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa . Agent with four tools (paper search, gather evidence, generate answer, citation traversal); every retrieved chunk is reranked and contextually summarized (RCS: 1 to 10 relevance score plus a 200 to 400 token summary) before answering. LitQA2: 248 multiple-choice questions whose answers sit in paper bodies, with an explicit "insufficient information" option; scored as precision (correct when answered) and accuracy (correct over all). PaperQA2 precision 85.2% vs humans 73.8%, accuracy 66.0% vs 67.7%, abstaining 21.9%. Ablations: removing RCS hurt accuracy (p < 0.001), ranking depth above 10 was "crucial", citation traversal raised DOI recall (p = 0.022), Grobid full-text parsing did not beat PyMuPDF. A Zotero loader exists (paperqa.contrib.ZoteroDB) but the docs call it untested lately: https://docs.edisonscientific.com/paperqa/docs/tutorials/where_do_i_get_papers
2. **OpenScholar / ScholarQABench** (AI2 and UW, 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14199 , https://allenai.org/blog/openscholar . 45M-paper datastore, Contriever bi-encoder (top 100) then BGE cross-encoder reranker; self-feedback loop (draft, up to three feedback notes, targeted re-retrieval, revise, then post hoc citation insertion for unsupported claims). Citation quality scored as Cite-p / Cite-r / Cite-F1. GPT-4o without retrieval hallucinated citations 78 to 90% of the time (94.8% in biomedicine). Successor suite AstaBench: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21652
3. **STORM** (Stanford, NAACL 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14207 . Pre-writing stage with multi-perspective simulated question-asking against a grounded expert, outline first; editors flagged source-bias transfer and over-association of unrelated facts.
4. **CiteME / CiteAgent** (Bethge lab, NeurIPS 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12861 . Given a claim excerpt, name the single paper it cites. Frontier LLMs 4.2 to 18.5%, humans 69.7%, search-equipped CiteAgent 35.3%.
5. **Self-RAG** (UW and AI2, ICLR 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511 . Reflection tokens for "retrieve or not", "is the passage relevant", "is the output supported". Miguel's tiered rule is a prompt-level analogue; the transferable idea is support scored per sentence, not per answer.
6. **MedRAG / MIRAGE** (Xiong et al., ACL Findings 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13178 . 7,663 medical QA items over 41 corpus x retriever x LLM combinations; RAG gained up to 18% over chain-of-thought; the best setup combined corpora and retrievers (BM25 plus dense), with lost-in-the-middle effects.
7. **Clinfo.ai** (Stanford Shah lab, PSB 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16146 , https://github.com/som-shahlab/clinfo.ai . Query-time PubMed retrieval then synthesis; released PubMedRS-200, a benchmark derived from systematic reviews.
8. **Almanac** (Stanford, NEJM AI 2024). https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10187428/ . Guideline-grounded RAG over 130 clinical scenarios; a physician panel scored factuality (+18%), completeness and safety.
9. **DeepResearch Bench and deep-research agents.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763 , https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14240 . 100 PhD-level tasks; scoring combines report quality with effective citation count and citation accuracy.
10. **Commercial tools.** Elicit (vendor-reported 94 to 99% extraction accuracy, https://elicit.com/blog/how-we-evaluated-elicit-systematic-review ; an independent study found accuracy varied with prompt wording and updates, https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/view/9909/ ). Consensus (staged semantic plus keyword retrieval with quality reranking). Scite Reference Check (retractions, editorial notices, DOI matching, https://help.scite.ai/en-us/article/how-do-i-use-the-scite-reference-check-xo6fv5/ ) and an Assistant that admits "citations that don't fully support the claim" ( https://scite.ai/blog/research-assistant-shows-its-work ). Undermind iterative search (recall claims vendor-only, https://www.undermind.ai/whitepaper.pdf ).
11. **Zotero-integrated assistants.** https://github.com/54yyyu/zotero-mcp (reads zotero.sqlite directly; full-text and ChromaDB semantic search; PDF page images where text extraction garbles math; annotation writing; works with Claude Code) and https://github.com/lifan0127/ai-research-assistant (Aria, a GPT-4 Zotero 6 plugin). No evaluation for either.
12. **ChemCrow, Coscientist, BioChatter.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05376 , https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06792-0 , https://biochatter.org . Tool-using lab agents rather than literature QA; BioChatter's continuous community benchmark is the transferable piece.
13. **Causal inference and epidemiology specific.** CauSciBench (ICML 2026, 300+ tasks; method selection is the bottleneck) https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/acharya26a.html ; CLadder (NeurIPS 2023) https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/631bb9434d718ea309af82566347d607-Abstract.html ; CausalNHANES preprint https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/109941 ; LLM E-value and Cornfield sensitivity analysis https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14273 ; CausalChat (GPT-4 recall, no retrieval) https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14146 ; Kleper and Melamed on confounding in target trial emulation https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.09.26352820 . All test reasoning or estimation; none tests literature-grounded methodology QA.
14. **Hallucinated-citation studies and checkers.** Walters and Wilder 2023 (55% GPT-3.5 vs 18% GPT-4 fabricated; 24% of real GPT-4 citations had substantive errors) https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10484980/ ; Chelli 2024 (28.6% GPT-4, 91.4% Bard) https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e53164 ; Linardon 2025 (19.9% fabricated overall; 6% for a high-awareness disorder vs 28 to 29% for low-awareness ones; 45% of real citations had DOI errors) https://mental.jmir.org/2025/1/e80371 ; GhostCite (14 to 95% across 13 LLMs) https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06718 ; Naser (11 to 57% across 10 LLMs, verified against Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar) https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03299 ; Badalova and Mayr (five reference checkers limited by extraction errors and database coverage) https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22693 .

## Prioritized improvements the survey justifies

1. Report precision and accuracy separately, with an explicit "insufficient evidence in corpus" option in the sealed probe (PaperQA2, LitQA2). Hours.
2. Add a "specialized vs general topic" stratum beside fame; fabrication rose from 6% to 29% with lower topic visibility (Linardon; Naser). Hours.
3. Rerank-and-summarize step after search: score each hit 1 to 10 and summarize before answering; keep depth above 10 (PaperQA2 ablations). A day.
4. Automatic existence and metadata check for any [recalled, unverified] citation via Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar before output, flagging DOI mismatches (Naser; Linardon; Badalova and Mayr). Hours.
5. Draft, critique, re-retrieve, revise, then per-claim citation check (OpenScholar self-feedback; Self-RAG support scoring). Loop is cheap; automated Cite-p/Cite-r scoring is medium.
6. Corpus-internal CiteME task: mask citations in CAUSALab papers and ask Miguel to name the cited paper (CiteME). Cheap to build, medium to run at scale.
7. Hybrid retrieval (FTS5 plus embeddings plus cross-encoder rerank) and citation traversal over reference lists (MedRAG; OpenScholar; PaperQA2). Weeks.
8. Page images for equation-heavy passages; write verified quotes back as Zotero annotations (zotero-mcp). Days.
9. Supporting/contrasting labels and retraction check per cited paper (Scite; PaperQA2 contradiction detection). Costly, lower priority.

## Could not verify

Undermind recall figures (vendor whitepaper only). Lancet/Topaz 2026 fabricated-reference audit seen only via secondary coverage. CausalNHANES is a preprint with implausible model names. Elicit accuracy is vendor-reported. Scite detecting fabricated references is a blog claim. "ZotGPT" was not found. Kleper and Melamed results not read beyond the abstract. OpenScholar's Nature publication known only from UW news. Linardon's "two-thirds" headline does not match the reported counts (about 56%).
