Verified verbatim against Hernan & Robins *What If* (21nov25 PDF), Fine Point 22.5 (printed p.317, PDF page 326) and Ch 22.4 (printed p.315-316, PDF 324-325). Companion to [[whatif-ccw-grace-period-citations]] and [[cef-metro-itt-design]]. Built for the Clalit BSI empiric-antibiotic per-protocol facet work.

**Why a grace period exists (the clinical-realism argument)** - FP22.5 verbatim: "antiretroviral therapy cannot be started exactly on the same day that it is assigned... it may take weeks or months until the requisite clinical and administrative procedures are completed and patients are adequately informed. Therefore, investigators need to define a grace period... Otherwise the study would be estimating the effect of strategies that do not occur frequently in reality or that could not be successfully implemented in practice." => the grace length is justified by what is OPERATIONALLY ACHIEVABLE in routine care, NOT by an arbitrary analytic window. This is the lever for asking a clinician how long the empiric decision realistically stays open.

**Grace redefines the estimand to per-protocol, NOT ITT** - FP22.5 verbatim: "When using grace periods with cloning and censoring, the intention-to-treat effect cannot be estimated because almost everyone will contribute a clone to each of the treatment strategies... analyses with grace period at baseline are geared towards estimating some form of per-protocol effect."

**MUST specify timing of initiation within the grace window** - FP22.5 final line verbatim: "a well-defined initiation strategy with a grace period should specify the timing of initiation during the grace period. For details, see the Appendix in Cain et al. (2010)." => Two strategies with the same grace window G but different within-window timing distributions (e.g. uniform vs all-at-hour-0) are DIFFERENT estimands. If real cefuroxime and ceftriaxone initiation-time distributions differ within G, the per-protocol contrast must either (a) hold the within-window timing distribution common across arms, or (b) be explicit that it targets "initiate-by-G under the natural/observed timing." This is the clinical fact the interview must surface (how fast is the first dose actually given, and does it differ by drug).

**Point/initiation PP vs sustained PP** - a grace-period "initiate within G" strategy is a POINT intervention with a waiting window; after G the treatment is unconstrained (the v2 estimand, weight frozen after G). A SUSTAINED strategy ("initiate AND continue the regimen through period P") is a time-varying treatment requiring a full longitudinal IPCW over P (Ch 21 g-methods; SEQTaRget excused/deviation flags per follow-up interval). For EMPIRIC antibiotic therapy that is, by clinical design, expected to be revised once cultures return, the sustained-through-the-stay PP is arguably ILL-POSED as a target (it would penalize guideline-concordant de-escalation as "deviation"); the defensible sustained estimand stops at the reassessment point (culture-result time), making the empiric window itself the natural protocol period. The grace length and the reassessment point are therefore the same clinical quantity viewed twice.

**Single eligibility time => single-origin CCW, not sequential** - Ch 22.4 (PDF 325): eligibility met at a single time (here the one empiric-decision moment per encounter) => one time-zero; the sequential multi-trial machinery (strategy c, emulate a new trial at each eligible time) collapses to one trial per encounter. SEQTaRget is still usable as single-trial enrollment but its sequential efficiency gain does not apply.
